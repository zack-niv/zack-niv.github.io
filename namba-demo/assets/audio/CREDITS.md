# Audio credits

## Crowd recordings (`crowd/*.mp3`)

Each loop is a 20 s excerpt of a field recording by **maciej janasik (via radio aporee)**, published on the Internet
Archive. The licence for all three is **Public Domain Mark 1.0** (<http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/>);
that is the `licenseurl` shown on each Internet Archive listing, which is where this licence statement comes from.
Attribution is not legally required for public domain material; credit is given as a courtesy. The files were
level-adjusted, cut into seamless loops and re-encoded as MP3.

| `station_*` | Osaka Station (Umeda), Kita Ward, Osaka; recorded 2013-03-31 18:53 JST | "Osaka Station, escalator by baggage lockers - escalator voice" | <https://archive.org/details/aporee_17271_20110> (file: <https://archive.org/download/aporee_17271_20110/esc.mp3>) |

Licence, as recorded in the project's source notes: "Public Domain Mark 1.0 (the item's licenseurl field on
archive.org)". Caveat from the same notes: the Public Domain Mark is an assertion that the work is already free of
known copyright; it is not a waiver by the creator, and the uploader (radio aporee) did not state CC0.

## Announcement and other voices (`voice/*.mp3`)

Announcement and other spoken lines are AI-generated speech (Google Gemini TTS, voice "Zephyr" for announcements;
other Google voices for staff and passers-by). No human voice recording is used.

## Music, chimes and effects

Music, chimes, jingles and sound effects are synthesized by the game itself (`js/audio/`); they are not recordings.

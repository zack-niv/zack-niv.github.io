## Contracts

**Place links (item 5).**

- **Phone API:** `ctx.phone.message({ …, place: slotId })` renders a link-preview card inside the bubble:
  - thumbnail/category glyph, name, floor, open/closed, distance as seen by the active app;
  - an "Open in Maps" / "Open in Lodestone" action.
- **Tapping:** with the phone up, tap or press the card's key. It opens the active nav app with that place selected (the
  place card, then Go) and emits `nav:destination` with `{ app, suggested: true, via: 'link' }`.
- **Aya's texts:** she sends cards for Café Mitsubachi, Tempura Daikichi and the Lodestone install link (the existing
  link card stays).
- **Destination list:** the "From Aya" pick in the list remains.

**Milestones (item 1).** `ctx.phone.nextMilestone` returns
`{ kind: 'escalator'|'stairs'|'lift'|'passage'|'gate'|'turn'|'arrive', dir: 'up'|'down'|null, toLevel, dist, name? }`,
emitted as `nav:milestone` on change.

## Quality bar

- **1**
  - **Glance strip and Lodestone instruction card:** always show a big heading arrow pointing where to walk now, as in
    v3 track.js, together with the next milestone as a smaller icon and text.
    - Example: `↗ (arrow) · then [escalator icon] Escalator down to 1F · 165 m`.
    - Under 30 m, the milestone takes over with "Escalator down — on your left".
  - **Long legs (>60 m):** add a landmark cue from the directory ("Straight past Sneaker Lab, 120 m") so the player
    always knows what they'll pass next.
  - **Readability:** one glance answers which way, what's next, and how far.
  - **Off-route:** the amber/off states still win.
- **2:** boarding is continuous.
  - The agent walks the last ~1–1.5 m to the lane start along a smooth path.
  - Yaw eases to the lane direction, speed blends into the step speed, and the walk→ride clip cross-fades
    (about 0.3 s).
  - Same in reverse at the exit landing.
  - Nobody pops, rotates instantly or slides sideways. Verify near-LOD at 2–4 m by sampling position and yaw per frame
    across boarding (no step over ~0.15 m/frame at 60 fps-equivalent; yaw rate ≤ ~360°/s).
- **4:**
  - Prefer making the canyon the natural way up: route the guided path *through* the canyon's own outdoor stairs or
    escalators toward the Parks upper floors, with no out-and-back.
  - If the nav graph can't, give it purpose: Aya asks for one photo from the canyon garden ("send me a pic of the
    canyon 🌿📸"). The player raises the phone there and takes it (validated), Aya reacts, then the route continues.
  - Never a pointless back-track. Lodestone's step text should say why ("Through the canyon garden — the scenic way up").
- **5:** see Contracts. Link cards must look like a real messaging app's preview: rounded card, glyph or photo-tile,
  2 lines max.

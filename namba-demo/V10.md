## Design principle: reality creates the need, not a timer

1. **Aya knows how long the walk should take.** For the current target (café or Daikichi), she uses
   `expected = route metres / 1.25 m/s + slack` (slack ≈ 60 s + 20 s per floor change), taken from the nav-graph
   distance at the time the leg starts.
   - She stays quiet while the player is within the expected time and making progress.
   - Check-ins come when the player is **behind expectation** (elapsed > expected × 1.0–1.2) or **wandering**
     (progress lost by the v9 rules, measured against the current target).
   - There are no fixed-clock beats.
2. **Maps is the antagonist, not Aya.** She reacts to what is really happening, with specific, human lines:
   - on the wrong floor for 20 s or more: "why are you on 2F?? 😂 I'm on 6F";
   - stalled for 25 s or more near the start or the gates: "still at the station?";
   - wandering away: "wait where are you going 😅".

   Use at most 2 of these per leg, and keep them under the v9 patience and turn rules.
3. **Location beats** (one each, deferred while a question is fresh, dropped if stale):
   - entering Namba CITY on the latte leg: a light tip ("café's on 1F 👇 near the …"; use the real directory/landmark);
   - at the café: nothing new; the order flow stays;
   - entering Namba Parks (2F/3F) on the Daikichi leg: see 4.
4. **Namba Parks is the honest trigger for good players.** A player making good progress who has not been offered
   Lodestone gets it on entering Parks: "ok Parks is a maze, even I got lost 😅 use this →".
   - This is realistic (Parks has 6+ floors, the canyon and split escalators) and lands the upgrade at the moment of
     maximum need.
   - Every player meets Lodestone. Lost or slow players get it earlier via "I'm lost", the behind-expectation check-in
     going unanswered, or "Which way??".
5. **The safety net is not a stopwatch.**
   - The hard cap stays: offer ≤ 240 s (was 210) only as a never-stall guard. It should almost never fire for a moving
     player; log when it does.
   - The analytics `demo:offer.why` gains `parks` and `behind`.

## Quality bar

- **`storysim`:** all existing scenarios still pass, plus new ones: strong player (fast, never lost), slow walker,
  wrong-floor wanderer, silent stander. For each, the offer `why` and the time are as the design predicts.
- **`walk.mjs` personas** (normal, `REPLY=none`, `REPLY=instant`, `REPLY=decline`, `&wander`): each arrives with
  `ctx.errors []`, the chip invariant at 0 bad and the offer ≤ 240 s.
  - The bot (a strong player) should now get the offer via `parks`, not a timer.
  - Report every beat with its trigger reason.
- **No fixed-clock beat fires for a moving player.** List every timer-driven fire in each run; the expected count is
  zero.
- **Aya's thread reads like a real friend.** Specific to where you are, never contradicting the player's state. Quote
  the threads.
- **Before-phase length for a strong player.** Report it. The Maps frustration should still be felt, and the end card's
  Before has enough samples (at least 20 s of walking).

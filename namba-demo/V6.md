## Contracts

**Gates → Story**

- **Calls:** `ctx.transit.gateLaneNear(x, z, level)` → `{ gate, lane, dir, ok } | null`.
  `ctx.transit.tapGate(gate, lane)` opens that lane for the player for ~3 s and returns `{ ok, reason }`.
- **Events:**
  - `gate:tap { gate, lane, x, z }`
  - `gate:pass { …existing }`
  - `gate:blocked { gate, lane }`: the player walked into a lane without tapping. The flaps snap shut with the beep;
    the player is physically stopped and must step back and tap.
- **NPCs** keep auto-tapping, with a visible wrist-tap.
- **Fares:** the existing IC fare logic in `js/game/game.js` (gate:pass handling) moves to the tap.

**Story: shared IC purchases**

- `ctx.game.icCharge({ amount, label, ja, kind: 'fare'|'purchase' })` → `{ ok, balance }`.
- It drives the existing `hud.ic` chip.
- Used by gates, café orders, vignettes and machines, so there is one mechanism.

**Phone: realism and metrics**

- `positioning.js` error in Maps follows real indoor GPS: ±10–30 m, worst underground and near tall structure, with
  wrong-floor episodes and dot hops.
- `stats()` adds fair metrics, so the end card can show for example:
  - mean and p90 position error;
  - % of time on the wrong floor;
  - wrong turns or reroutes per km;
  - metres walked per metre of real progress (detour factor);
  - time to first correct heading after a turn.

  Story chooses the 3–4 that read best.

## Quality bar

- **1:** "Where are you??" no earlier than ~90–100 s, and only when the player has clearly stalled or wandered (or
  a later fallback). Never while they are making clear progress.
- **2:** gates look like real Japanese automatic ticket gates:
  - slim cabinets, the IC reader pad lit blue, coloured flaps that pop out and retract, entry and no-entry LEDs, a
    fare display on the exit side;
  - the tap gives a beep plus a light flash plus the IC chip with fare or balance;
  - an untapped walk-in gets the flaps snapping shut plus a buzzer, and the player is stopped.

  The tutorial's E step is *tap your IC card at the gate* (teaching E). Its hint sits at the gate, and it's validated by
  `gate:tap`.

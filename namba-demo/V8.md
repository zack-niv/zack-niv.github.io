## Hypotheses (verify first)

- **1:** `js/ui/phone/positioning.js` Maps mode already models ±10–30 m error, hops and wrong-floor episodes (v6), but the
  dot still *tracks* the player's motion with noise added, so it feels like a bad GPS rather than no GPS. Real indoor
  behaviour has three states:
  - a **stale fix**: the dot frozen at the last outdoor or entrance fix for tens of seconds while the player walks;
  - a **coarse Wi-Fi or cell estimate** that jumps 20–60 m to a wrong place (sometimes on the wrong floor) and sits there;
  - a **brief good fix** near big openings.

  The accuracy circle grows while stale. The heading comes from the compass alone (noisy near steel).
- **2:** the v7 PA changes (`announcer.js`: `PA_DUCK`, `PLAT_CHIME`, louder speech on the platform) may not reach the
  Japanese line. Possible causes: the formant or TTS voice gain, a zone attenuation, or the mixer's speech bus. Measure the
  actual output level of the JA line against the beds with `ctx.audio.debug()` before changing gains.
- **3:** the full app shows a large instruction card on top, a small isometric 3D stack, and the place card at the bottom.
  The map itself is the least helpful part: a tiny, abstract stack. Lean fix:
  - a real top-down map of the current floor (the Maps app's renderer, `maprender.js`, is the base) with the route line,
    your dot plus heading cone, the next milestone pin and nearby landmark names, following you heading-up;
  - the **same header component** as the glance/lowered strip (arrow, distance, milestone, landmark cue) used in both
    modes, so the phone reads the same raised or lowered;
  - the 3D stack stays as the toggle (V) for multi-floor context.
- **4:** the calibration screen is abstract (random field loops) and the copy is jargon. Make it a clear, short, staged
  progress screen, e.g.:
  - "Reading the magnetic field ✓"
  - "Matching it to Namba's indoor map ✓"
  - "Finding your floor… **2F** ✓"
  - "You're here · ±1 m"

  Plus one simple visual (a compass or dot converging onto the floor plan), and the floor bar highlighting the floor
  found. Same timing (no slower).
- **5:** the platform escalator's foot lands next to a gate line. `gateLaneNear` (or the gate-hint logic in
  `game.js` / `story.js`) fires on proximity alone, without checking that the player is on the paid side, approaching a
  lane, or that the lane is ahead. Verify the exact spot, then gate the prompt on facing a lane within ~1.5 m and actually
  moving toward it.
- **6:** at start, boarding NPCs walk to the car's centre or a fixed offset rather than the nearest **door** position of
  the stopped train (door offsets per car in `transit/cars.js` / `trains.js`), or they board before the train has
  stopped. Verify with a screenshot at start; fix the target to door positions (and only once the doors are open).
- **7:** `index.html`'s `#loading` shows なんば + LOST IN NAMBA. Something (`core/loader.js` or `ui/title.js`) swaps in a
  "Namba" heading when progress starts. One consistent loading screen whose title is "Lost in Namba" (Japanese なんば as
  the small accent at most), unchanged from first paint to the title screen.

  - Walking 60 m indoors with Maps, the dot is frozen or wrong for most of it.
  - At least one jump of 25 m or more to a wrong place, and one wrong-floor episode in a typical 2–3 min Maps phase.
  - Never so broken that Maps' destination list and directions stop working.
  - The tutorial (pick a place in Maps) still works.

### Item 1 — what the screenshots show (find the shared pattern)

- **(a) Namba CITY B1/1F hall, Sennichimae/Midosuji signs.** Shelving, boxes and
  book rows hang upside-down from the ceiling. We see the floor above's shop
  furniture from below, through a ceiling or slab that isn't drawn or is
  one-sided.
- **(b) Parks 6F wood-deck corridor.** A restaurant's back room ("関係者以外立入禁止",
  厨房) has no outer wall. The room reads as an open box from the corridor,
  probably a v2 "shortened deep room" whose back wall only faces inwards and
  leaves the rest of the slot exposed.
- **(c) Looking down from a Parks terrace onto the dining floors.** The
  restaurants have no roofs or outer walls: whole furnished interiors float
  like dollhouses.

Hypothesis: shop and room geometry assumes it is only ever seen from inside
the corridor. New sight lines (wells, shortened rooms, open canyon and
terraces, the restored ceilings) expose its back, top and underside. Every
room needs a closed, outward-facing shell (outer walls, a roof or ceiling
cap, a slab underside), and leftover slot volume needs filling. Verify the
cause before fixing, and sweep the whole route for the same class of bug.

## Contracts

**Destinations (Phone provides, Story uses).**

```js
ctx.phone.suggest(slotId | null)      // highlight a suggested place in both apps' lists/search ("Aya's pick")
ctx.phone.destination                 // current { slotId, name, level } or null
ctx.phone.setDestination(slotId)      // programmatic; Story should rarely need it
// events:
'nav:destination' { slotId, name, app: 'maps'|'lodestone', suggested: bool }   // player picked one
'nav:arrived'     { slotId }                                                  // reached the chosen destination
```

- **Both apps:** a destination list (search box, categories, plus "From Aya"
  suggestions at the top) built from `js/world/directory.js` (FEATURED and
  `searchBusinesses`). Lodestone gains the same list.
- **No auto-routing:** neither app routes until the player picks a
  destination.
- **Lodestone after install:** keeps the destination already picked in Maps
  (if any), so the upgrade moment still snaps straight into guidance.

**Coffee checkpoint (Story).** Aya asks the player to grab her a coffee on
the way, e.g. *"can you bring me an iced latte from Wakakusa? 🙏 the queue here
is forever"*.

- **Where:** Wakakusa (slot `link_e03`) or another café off the straight line,
  so the player makes a couple of turns and uses the destination list twice.
- **How:** reuse the v2 order mechanic (`demo:order {slotId}`) and
  `ctx.phone.suggest`.
- **Order:** the coffee step can come before or after the Lodestone upgrade.
  Story decides; the upgrade moment must still land within the first ~3–4 min
  for a typical player.
- **Payoff:** arrival at Daikichi with the coffee gets a small payoff from
  Aya. Without it she teases you, and the game never blocks.
- **End card:** stats must stay sane across a two-leg trip.

## Quality bar

- **1** No floating furniture, roofless rooms or open box backs from any
  reachable vantage point on the route: Nankai, CITY B1/1F/2F, the Parks
  bridge, the canyon terraces, 2F–6F and the 6F deck. Fix the cause, not
  individual rooms. Perf neutral, so cap with cheap merged geometry.
- **2** Phone prop always in hand whenever a phone/photo clip plays, near LOD
  and far LOD. No exceptions.
- **3** Seated people sit *on* the seat:
  - hips at seat height, facing the table or counter, thighs horizontal,
    shins down, feet on the floor or footrest;
  - no legs through chairs, no sideways bodies;
  - stools at a counter face the counter;
  - "eating" is a subtle arm and head loop at the table, not a contortion.
  - Hide any seated pose that can't be made to fit, rather than show it broken.
- **Clump** No dense standing crowd at escalator heads. People queue single
  file into the lanes, and overflow takes the stairs or waits spread out.
  Check the Nankai 3F head right at the start, when the rapi:t passengers
  arrive.
- **4/6**
  - The tutorial includes "choose where to go" in Maps, then again for the
    coffee or tempura leg.
  - The suggested place is highlighted but free choice is allowed. If the
    player picks something else, Aya reacts lightly and the game still works.
- **5** In free roam after the end card, Lodestone navigates to any
  directory place.

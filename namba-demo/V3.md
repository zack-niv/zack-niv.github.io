## Contracts

**Messages with replies (Phone provides, Story uses).**

```js
ctx.phone.message({ id, from: 'Aya', text, link?, replies?: [{ id, text }], expectReply?: true })
// → shows in Messages (glance strip notifies; unread badge). If replies are given, the
//   thread shows reply chips; the player answers with phone up (click, or keys 1/2/3).
// → emits 'phone:reply' { msgId, replyId, text } and appends the player's bubble.
// 'phone:typing' { from, on } — Story can show "Aya is typing…" before a message.
ctx.phone.openApp('messages' | 'maps' | 'lodestone')   // emits 'phone:app' { app }
ctx.phone.app                                           // current app id
```

The existing `phone:message` event keeps working. **The phone must no longer
self-trigger the Lodestone offer on a timer** (`_play > 150` in phone.js):
Story decides when Aya offers it. Keep `offerLodestone()` as the call Story uses.

**Tutorial hooks (Phone + Story).** The phone emits `phone:pose` (exists),
`phone:app`, `phone:reply`, `phone:stack` {open} (3D view toggled). Story
listens and validates tutorial steps.

**Off-route signal (Phone → Story, optional).** Lodestone emits
`nav:track { state: 'on'|'drifting'|'off'|'rerouted', headingErr }` so Story can
have Aya react once ("wrong way? 😅") — at most once, never nagging.

**Caption channels (Story owns hud).** `hud.caption(text, { channel })` with
`channel: 'ambient'` (station PA, background voices — small, top-left/edge,
low contrast, auto-fades, never stacks more than 1–2) vs `'speech'` (a person
addressing you: "すみません") and `'action'` (E interactions, ordering) — bottom
centre, prominent. Sound/Crowd pass the channel where they call captions
(Crowd+Sound: update your call sites; default when absent = 'ambient' for PA,
'speech' for crowd excuse-me lines).

## Quality bar

- **1 + 5 (the heart of v3).** The tutorial IS the opening of the story; no
  separate card deck. Example flow (Story designs the real one):
  arrival on the rapi:t platform → phone buzzes → *"Raise your phone (Q)"*
  (validated by `phone:pose up`) → Aya: "Landed? 🍤" with reply chips → player
  replies (validated) → Aya: "Meet me at Tempura Daikichi, Parks 6F" → open
  Maps (validated) → *"Walk (WASD) — follow the signs"* (validated by moving
  N m) → … Each step waits for the player, shows one small contextual hint
  (in-world style, not a modal), and has a gentle nudge after ~10–15 s of
  inaction. Location triggers (left the platform, reached the gates, wrong
  floor for 40 s, wandering) drive Aya's later texts, and *the Lodestone offer
  needs the player to have engaged* (e.g. replied "I'm lost" or been clearly
  lost and answered her). Aya should feel like a real friend texting: typing
  indicator, short messages, emoji, reacts to where you are ("you're at the
  Nankai gates? go up, not down"). The player can always ignore the phone and
  explore; nothing hard-blocks movement. Skippable for replays (`?notutorial`).
- **2** A home/app switcher reachable in one action (e.g. swipe-up bar / a dock
  with Messages · Maps · Lodestone, plus keys 1-3 or Tab cycling while up —
  coordinate with reply keys). Unread badges.
- **3** Speak Japanese PA lines only (no English TTS). Captions still show the
  English translation as text.
- **4** No head spinning ever. Clamp head yaw relative to the body (±70°),
  wrap angles correctly, smooth with a rate limit. Check near and far LODs.
- **6** Pause menu: "Restart" → "Are you sure? Your progress will be lost" →
  full clean restart (reload with the right flags is fine).
- **7** From any approach, an escalator well must read as leading somewhere:
  the destination level visible through the opening (render adjacent levels
  near voids / portals), a lit landing, ceiling opening matching the well;
  never a flat ceiling over the top or a speckled floor at the bottom. If full
  rendering is too costly, use a convincing cheap technique (e.g. render the
  next level within a radius of the void, or a baked/impostor view of the
  landing + light spill), but it must not look like a bug. Check every
  escalator on the route (Nankai 3F↔2F, CITY, Parks 2F→6F).
- **8** See caption channels above.
- **9** Footsteps −6 dB or so, softer attack; still audible on hard floors.
- **10** Heading-aware guidance in Lodestone: the instruction arrow rotates
  with the player's facing relative to the route; on track = calm blue;
  drifting (heading > ~60° off or 5 m off the path) = amber + "Turn around" /
  "Turn left" hint; off (> ~12 m or wrong floor) = reroute with a short phone
  vibration (CSS shake on the glance card + a soft buzz sound via
  `ctx.audio` if available) and a new route. The glance strip must show this
  too so it works with the phone down. Subtle, never spammy (hysteresis).

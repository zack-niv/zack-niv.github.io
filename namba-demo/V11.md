## Design notes and hypotheses

- **1:**
  - The share card must sell the premise: lost in a huge real station-mall maze, a friend waiting at a tempura place
    on 6F.
  - It must not show Lodestone, the 3D stack or "magnetic". Re-render og.jpg (1200×630) from the game, e.g. a
    first-person concourse with signs and crowds, plus the phone showing Aya's "Meet me at Tempura Daikichi, 6F…" or a
    useless Maps dot ("No GPS signal").
  - Copy direction: the quest, not the twist, e.g. "Six floors, four stations, one tempura lunch. Can you find it?"
  - Update og/twitter descriptions and the page description.
  - Check the title screen and loading copy for the same spoiler ("magnetic fingerprint" etc.).
  - Copy is Zack's to approve: propose 2–3 taglines in notes and ship the best.
- **3: Aya has NO shared location.** Fix the root, not the lines.
  - Every check-in may only use what a friend could know:
    - time elapsed vs how long the walk should take;
    - what the player told her;
    - photos the player sent.
  - Lines like "your dot isn't getting any closer" and "wrong floor?" asserted as a fact are out. Use questions
    ("you're not getting any closer, are you? 😃", "where are you now?"), guesses ("probably still in the station
    maze lol") and asks.
  - Update the DEMO.md "How Aya knows where you are" note, which the lead wrote wrongly in v10.
  - v10's internal triggers (behind pace, wandering, wrong floor) may still decide *when* she checks in (she senses
    "it's taking long"), but her *words* must not reveal sensor knowledge.
- **2 + 3: the photo mechanic** (lead's proposal; design it lean).
  - Aya asks "send me a pic of something near you 📸 I'll tell you where you are".
  - A chip or key on the phone ("📸 Send photo") captures the current game view: a downscaled canvas snapshot after a
    shutter flash, shown as an image bubble in Messages.
  - Aya replies by recognising the spot, the way a local friend would: she has the player's TRUE location here,
    because the photo shows it, e.g. "omg that's the Nankai gates! go down the big escalator 👇" or "that's CITY 2F
    near Megane Square, the café's one floor down".
  - Use the real zone/landmark/directory names.
  - This is also the natural engagement beat for quiet stretches.
  - Also add light, realistic engagement texts during long quiet stretches. Rules:
    - at most one every ~45–60 s of no Aya contact while the player is moving;
    - never stack, never over an open question;
    - examples: "you ok? 😊", "the line just moved!! 🍤", "take your time, it's a maze lol", queue updates.
  - Max ~40 s of total silence while walking.
  - It must still feel like a friend, not a notification spammer.
- **5:**
  - Measure first: bed RMS vs PA speech by zone (platform, concourse, CITY, Parks).
  - Likely: the beds are simply too loud overall, and PA lines elsewhere (concourse, CITY "store announcements") are
    too quiet or rare.
  - Lower the bed levels globally (~−4 to −6 dB), duck harder under PA (the speech itself is speechSynthesis at max
    volume and can't go louder), and make sure announcements exist and are audible in each area.
  - Keep the vibe.
- **6: mobile/tablet.** `js/core/input.js` already has a touch layer (virtual stick, drag look, buttons) from the full
  game. Make the demo genuinely playable on phone and tablet in landscape:
  - a rotate-to-landscape prompt when portrait;
  - fullscreen on start where supported (iOS Safari has no Fullscreen API on iPhone; degrade gracefully);
  - FPS-style controls: a left virtual stick (floating, appears where the thumb lands), right-side drag look with
    tuned sensitivity, an E/"Use" button that appears only when an interaction is available, a phone button, and a run
    toggle;
  - the phone UI tappable: chips, dock, place cards, Lodestone buttons; tap targets ≥ 44 px;
  - performance: a mobile default quality tier (lower DPR, crowd count and shadows) so mid-range phones hold
    about 30 fps;
  - title and tutorial copy adapted for touch ("Drag to look · stick to walk", not "WASD");
  - the end card and contact links usable;
  - no pointer-lock logic in the way.
  - Test with Playwright mobile emulation (`isMobile`, `hasTouch`, 844×390 and 1180×820 landscape, 390×844 portrait for
    the prompt) and real touch events.
  - Analytics already records the device; add `input: 'touch'` to the super properties if not there (ask the lead;
    analytics.js is lead-owned).

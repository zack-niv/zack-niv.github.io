## Lead's diagnosis and hypotheses (verify first)

- **1. We lock the bars in ourselves.** `css/style.css` sets `html, body { overflow: hidden; touch-action: none }`.
  - iPhone Safari only hides its toolbars when the *document* scrolls, so they can never go away.
  - There is no element Fullscreen API on iPhone (confirmed on Zack's iOS: `goFullscreen()` ran and nothing happened).
    It works on iPad and Android: keep it.
  - **Build:**
    - On iPhone Safari only (touch, not standalone, no fullscreen API, bars showing), the title screen becomes a
      scrollable document a little taller than the viewport, with a small "↑ Swipe up for full screen" cue
      (Japanese line too, in house style).
    - When the bars collapse (`visualViewport.height` / `innerHeight` grows to about `screen` height in landscape), the
      cue fades and the page re-locks at that scroll position. Tap to begin must work at any time without swiping: no
      forced step.
    - In game, if the bars come back (tapping the top edge shows them), do NOT nag. At most, a one-time subtle chip
      in the pause menu.
    - Add a one-line "Play full screen: Share → Add to Home Screen" tip in Settings for iPhone only.
  - **Visible bug Zack didn't mention (shot 0):** with the bars showing (about 270 px of visible height),
    `.t-main` (bottom-anchored) grows upward over `.t-place` ("南海なんば駅 · Nankai Namba") and pushes なんば off
    the top. The title must fit the *visible* height (`--vh` already exists in touch.js; `#view` uses `100vh`, which on
    iOS is the large viewport). Fit it at 844×260, 844×330 and 844×390.
- **2a. Cramped HUD and menu (shots 2–4):**
  - PA captions are huge on a phone (two stacked boxes, about 40% of the screen, over the WALK stick). One caption at a
    time, smaller type, max about 45% width, top-left under the safe area, never over the stick.
  - The tutorial hint pill runs under the Run button and gets clipped ("open Maps and pick where to…"). It must sit
    clear of the button cluster and wrap or shorten.
  - The pause menu at 852×393 is desktop typography squeezed: the contact cards fall off the bottom. Use a phone layout:
    smaller headline, tighter rows, the right column scrolls, and the cards stay visible or reachable.
  - The lowered-phone peek card ("Pick a place in Maps") is big; make it more compact.
  - The touch gate hint wrap ("walk up to a lane, then 🖐") is a v11 leftover.
- **2b. The phone is a portrait device on a 393 px-tall landscape screen.** It gets about 45% of the width, and inside
  it the status bar, notification banner, header, compass toast, chips, place card and dock leave the map about 80 px.
  This is a design problem; the lead's hypothesis, to be challenged:
  - On a short landscape screen, the raised phone turns sideways (a landscape phone, like people do with Maps). It is
    wide (about 70–80% of the screen width) and short, laid out side-by-side: the map or 3D on the left, the
    sheet/list/chips on the right, and the dock as a slim rail.
  - Messages can stay as a narrower column.
  - Alternative to weigh: keep it portrait but collapse the chrome (no status bar row, the banner as a slim strip, toasts
    inside the map, chips scroll).
  - Pick by screenshots at 844×390 and 852×393. The Lodestone reveal hero and the 3D stack must be *better* than now,
    not just fit.
  - Also seen in shot 1: the "Compass needs calibration" toast covers the header and "Where to?"; the notification
    banner covers the map.
- **3. PA silent on iPhone, prime suspect:**
  - `audio.js` `kick` runs on the first `pointerdown` (capture), and `announcer.unlock()` sets `_unlocked = true`
    **before** knowing whether the silent utterance was accepted.
  - On iOS, `pointerdown` is not a user activation that WebKit accepts for `speechSynthesis` (it wants touchend/click).
    The unlock is spent on a rejected gesture and never retried, so every later `speak()` is silently dropped, while
    Web Audio still resumes on the later `touchend`/`click`. That fits "captions but no voice".
  - Also check:
    - the voice regex picks an installed iOS voice (Kyoko / O-ren / Otoya; "compact" variants), and `getVoices()` is
      empty until later on iOS;
    - `u.volume` is ignored on iOS;
    - speech is cancelled by `visibilitychange` or by our `cancelSpeech()` paths;
    - the Web Audio session ducks speech (Safari 16.4+ `navigator.audioSession.type`; consider `'playback'` so the
      silent switch doesn't mute Web Audio either);
    - the chime level on phone speakers.
  - Fix the root cause: unlock on `touchend`/`click`, mark it unlocked only on `onstart`/`onend` success, retry on later
    gestures, and re-unlock after a `speechSynthesis.cancel()` if iOS needs it.
  - We cannot run real iOS here, so make the device report the truth (contract below).

## Contracts

- **PA → lead (analytics):** `ctx.audio.announcer.stats()` returns
  `{ voices, ja, unlocked, unlockVia, attempts, started, ended, errors: [codes], lastErr }`. The lead adds a
  `namba_pa` event (the first spoken attempt's outcome, plus a summary at 90 s), so Zack's next iPhone play tells us
  what happened.
- **Shell ↔ Phone:** the touch HUD (`#touch-ui`: Pause, Run, Phone and the Use pill) keeps its positions while the phone
  is raised. If a landscape phone needs a different safe region, Phone sets `html.ph-wide` on `<html>` while the wide
  phone is up, and Shell positions the HUD buttons for that class.
- **Shell → Phone:** the visible height is `--vh` (touch.js). When the bars collapse, `resize` fires, and the phone
  layout must re-measure on `resize`.
